What Lilypad is

Software that lets you see and control your own computer from your own phone, and a server that introduces the two to each other. The server does not carry your screen or your input; see Privacy for what it does hold.

Your account and your devices

You are responsible for your password and for the phones and computers you add. Anyone holding an unlocked phone that is on your account can control the computers on it — that is the product working as intended, so treat that phone the way you treat the computer.

If you lose a device, remove it in "Your devices". That takes effect immediately: a removed device cannot sign in, cannot reach anything, and any session it has open ends.

What you must not do

  • Use Lilypad to reach a computer you do not own or are not authorised to control. This is the one rule that matters most, and breaking it is likely also a crime where you live.
  • Use it to break the law, or to harm, harass, or spy on anyone.
  • Attack the service — attempt to break into other people's accounts, disrupt the server, or work around rate limits and abuse controls.

Accounts doing any of the above can be suspended or deleted without notice. Reports of a security problem are welcome and will not be treated as an attack; write to the address below.

It can be unavailable, it can lose the ability to reach your computer, and features can change. Do not rely on it as the only way to reach a machine that matters, and keep another way in.

Lilypad is free while it is in this early stage, and no payment details are collected. If paid plans are introduced, the prices and what they cover will be published here first, and using your own network to reach your own computer will remain free.

Ending it
